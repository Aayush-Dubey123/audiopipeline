## What it is

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Text → natural speech" icon="document">
		Paste up to 30,000 characters of Hindi, Marathi (Devanagari) or English. It is chunked, synthesized and stitched into a single narration.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Zero-shot voice cloning" icon="seti:audio">
		A 10–30 s reference recording is enough. The pipeline cleans it and picks the best 10 s for the model.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Runs on a laptop" icon="laptop">
		Built and measured on an RTX 3050 6 GB laptop GPU, with one resident model and a single-GPU job queue.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Responsible by default" icon="approve-check">
		Consent is required for custom voices. Every output carries a watermark and AI-generated metadata tags.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
