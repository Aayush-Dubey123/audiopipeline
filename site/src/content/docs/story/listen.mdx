## What to listen for

| Moment | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Between sentences | Background hiss cuts to dead silence, then returns | Continuous room tone; the pause sounds like a breath |
| Pause lengths | Same fixed gap everywhere | Shorter after commas, longer after questions and paragraphs |
| Delivery | Even, careful, slightly flat | More natural rise and fall |
| Loudness | Swells in pauses | One steady level |

<Aside type="note">
	The red **dead silence** chip under each waveform counts milliseconds of perfect digital silence, the most reliable splice detector I found. The waveform is clickable: jump to any moment, then flip versions.
</Aside>

<Aside type="caution" title="What these samples can’t show">
	The biggest gains came from cleaning the **cloning reference** (fixes 1–2), which a built-in voice doesn’t use. Those were measured on the client voice: similarity 0.933 → 0.944 and digital silence 2,930 ms → 130 ms on a 69 s narration.
</Aside>
