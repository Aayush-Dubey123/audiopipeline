Chatterbox exposes three settings that shape the voice:

| Setting | What it does | Too low | Too high |
|---|---|---|---|
| **exaggeration** | Expressiveness / emotional intensity | Flat, “reading” voice | Over-acted, unstable |
| **cfg_weight** | How strongly generation follows the guidance | Loose, follows the reference’s pace and accent | Slow, over-articulated |
| **temperature** | Randomness when sampling speech tokens | Monotone, repetitive | Erratic |

I can’t judge a voice by ear for every combination, so I scored them.

## The scoring

Each combination generated the same three Hindi chunks with fixed seeds, scored on:

- **Speaker similarity** to the reference (the model’s own voice encoder).
- **Pitch movement**: how much the voice rises and falls, compared with the real speaker.
- **Pace**: onset rate (a syllable proxy) compared with the real speaker.
- **Failed chunks**, from the new chunk check.

<Aside type="danger" title="The best score was a trap">
	At exaggeration 0.65, one chunk in every run **babbled for 35–40 seconds** on text that should take about 13. The chunk check flagged it, and the babble had also inflated the pitch and pace scores. A setting that fails that often is not usable, however good its average looks.
</Aside>

## Seeds matter as much as settings

Re-running the two best stable options with a second set of random seeds:

| Settings | Similarity | Pitch movement (reference 5.53) | Failed chunks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0.5 / 0.4 / 0.75** (chosen) | 0.946 – 0.955 | 3.2 – 4.3 | **0 of 6** |
| 0.4 / 0.3 / 0.85 | 0.952 – 0.955 | 3.7 – 4.8 | 1 of 6 |

Changing only the seed moved the scores as much as changing the settings. The chosen values were the only ones with no failures, so they stayed.

## The real limit

Every stable setting is still a little flatter than the real speaker (about 30% less pitch movement), because the model takes speaking style from only 6–10 seconds of reference. The biggest remaining lever is **a better reference recording** (15–30 s of natural, expressive speech, no clipping, quiet room), not more tuning.
